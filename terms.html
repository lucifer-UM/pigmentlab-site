What Pigment Lab is

Pigment Lab is a personal media project that publishes short paint-mixing videos to social platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The software behind it is an automated publishing tool operated by its owner to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.

Use of the videos

Videos are provided for viewing on the platforms where they are posted, under each platform's own terms. You may share them using the platform's share features. Please do not re-upload them as your own work.

No accounts, no payments

This website does not offer user accounts, subscriptions, purchases or downloads. No service is provided to third parties through the publishing tool.

Accuracy

Colour results are produced by a pigment simulation and may differ from physical paint brands. Colour names come from an open community dataset. Content is provided as is, without warranties.
