Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Pigment Lab does not collect personal data from visitors or viewers. The publishing tool only accesses the owner's own social media accounts.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log standard server information such as IP addresses to operate the hosting service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Platform integrations

The Pigment Lab publishing tool connects to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through their official APIs using OAuth. It requests only the permissions needed to upload videos and read basic information about the owner's own accounts. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold. The tool does not read, store or process data about other users, followers, viewers or commenters.

Data retention and deletion

The only stored data are the owner's own access tokens and the videos the owner created. Tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings, and local copies are deleted on request. To request deletion of any data, email the address below.

Children

The site and videos are not directed at children under 13 and no data is knowingly collected from them.
