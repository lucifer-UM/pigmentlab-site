Pigment Lab

Two paints go in. One colour comes out. Can you guess it before the reveal?

Pigment Lab mixes real artist paints like Phthalo Blue, Cadmium Yellow and Permanent Rose in a dish, stirs them, and names the result with its exact hex code. The mixing follows real pigment physics, so what you see is what actual paint does, not a screen blend.
